# Single-Phase Transformer Design and Construction

# Project Overview
This project focuses on the design, construction, and performance evaluation of a single-phase step-down transformer. The transformer was designed for a 220 V AC primary supply and a nominal 35 V, 4 A secondary output.

The project combines theoretical calculations with practical fabrication and experimental testing to develop an understanding of transformer design and operation.

# Project Specifications
- **Transformer Type:** Single-phase step-down transformer
- **Primary Voltage:** 220 V AC
- **Nominal Secondary Voltage:** 35 V AC
- **Rated Output Current:** 4 A
- **Frequency:** 50 Hz
- **Core Type:** Laminated EI-core
- **Primary Winding:** Enamel-coated copper wire, 24 SWG
- **Secondary Winding:** Enamel-coated copper wire, 17 SWG

## Project Objectives
- Calculate the transformer core area and winding turns.
- Select appropriate wire gauges and insulation materials.
- Construct and assemble the transformer.
- Perform open-circuit and short-circuit tests.
- Evaluate transformer losses and efficiency.

# Testing and Results
The transformer was evaluated through open-circuit and short-circuit tests.

- **Measured no-load secondary voltage:** 35.76 V
- **Estimated core loss:** 3 W
- **Measured copper loss:** 25 W
- **Reported efficiency:** Approximately 80%
